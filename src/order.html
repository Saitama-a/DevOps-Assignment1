How to Place Your Order

Getting your favourite meal at Campus Bites is simple. Follow these steps to enjoy a quick and convenient experience.

Four Easy Steps

Step 1: Choose Your Items

Explore our food menu and daily deals. Decide what you would like to eat before placing your order.

Step 2: Place Your Order

Visit the cafeteria counter and clearly tell the cashier your selected items and quantities.

Step 3: Pay at the Counter

Confirm your order total and pay using an accepted payment method. Keep your receipt if one is provided.

Step 4: Collect Your Token and Meal

Collect your order token, wait for your number to be called, and collect your meal from the designated pickup counter.

Important Ordering Guidelines

  • Peak hours: Allow extra time during lunch breaks.
  • Pickup: Keep your token until you receive your order.
  • Order checking: Check your items before leaving the counter.
  • Queue: Wait patiently and respect other customers.
  • Special requests: Inform the cashier before payment.
